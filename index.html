The problem

Many conversations still depend on the right person being in the room.

For people with hearing deficiencies, everyday communication can become limited in public services, education, healthcare, and social settings. When an interpreter is unavailable, access becomes slower and more unequal.

schools
hospitals

Hearo is built for the places where a quick text translation can reduce friction immediately.

The product

Gesture, sensors, processing, text.

The glove reads finger movement through wearable sensors, processes the signal, and displays the interpreted result as text. It avoids the lighting and camera-position limits of video-only systems.

Validation

Presented internationally. Awarded first place.

The project was presented at the Abu Dhabi International Invention Exhibition, where it won 1st place. That validation supports the product's innovation, practical utility, and global potential.

Built with a real engineering workflow

  • Fusion 360 for wearable structure and 3D-printed casing.
  • KiCad for schematic and PCB design.
  • Arduino IDE, Node.js, Python, and JavaScript for signal processing.

Market edge

Rollout

Local trust first, then national visibility.

The fastest path is demonstration-heavy: show the glove in places where communication pressure is real, collect feedback, and improve after each event cycle.

Partnerships

Associations for people with hearing deficiencies, USV, schools, and airports.

Workshops

Local resources in Suceava, student demos, classroom pilots, and hands-on sessions.

Promotion

Events with city hall, open days, school fairs, live presentations, website, and social media.

Product lifecycle

A practical path from prototype to a better second version.

  1. Year 0 Prototype demos

    Short live sequences at fairs, expos, concerts, and school events show the glove's weight, response, and real use.

  2. Year 1 Growth after fairs and expos

    Collect feedback from associations, students, teachers, and public institutions; improve onboarding and presentation materials.

  3. Year 2 Growth after improvement

    Reduce cost under 1500 lei, raise accuracy toward 95%, and prepare for international competitions and pilots.
